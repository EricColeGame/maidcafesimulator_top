The named Maid Cafe Simulator roster has five maids: Omu, Mina, Yuki, Momo and Jennie ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/?l=english)). The store also mentions NPC roles such as a Maidchelin judge, a regular otaku, a Maidroid courier and a puppy, as distinct characters from the maid roster.

Joining milestones, latest values

- Yuki: joining story triggers when you talk to her in front of the shop the next morning, per the v1.21 change to the v1.20 trigger ([v1.21](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847286114035125)).
- Jennie and Momo: join through the 2-star Maidchelin stage, now requiring Cafe Lv11 (was Lv12 in v1.20, Lv13 before that) (v1.21; [v1.20](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846651706680712)).
- Omu and Mina are the two maids present in the demo; the demo covers the first four chapters and Cafe Level 10.

Notes

The store counts 36 bond stories and 13 main story chapters across all five maids. Individual personalities and backstories are not detailed in the official notes checked here.
