Maid Cafe Simulator's roster is five kemonomimi maids: Omu, Mina, Yuki, Momo and Jennie ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/?l=english)). The store lists five maids and 36 bond stories in total.

What is confirmed about each

- Yuki joins as the story progresses. Since v1.21, her joining story starts when you talk to her in front of the shop the next morning instead of when you go to sleep (v1.20 also changed this trigger) ([v1.20](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846651706680712), [v1.21](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847286114035125)).
- Jennie and Momo join through the 2-star Maidchelin stage. That requirement dropped to Cafe Lv12 in v1.20 and then Lv11 in v1.21, so they join earlier than at launch.
- Omu has exclusive DLC costumes (Black Sugar Bunny, Spring Sunshine Garden, Jersey Maid Uniform, Maidroid) ([DLC listings](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4786460/)).
- Mina has Maidroid and Starlight Diner DLC outfits ([DLC listings](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4817670/)).

Gift equipment

Maids can gift cooking stations, and higher-tier blenders, brewers and shakers come at deeper bond levels (v1.21).

Not documented

Personality descriptions, preferred gifts and individual bond routes are not detailed in the official notes checked here. Bond story content is not confirmed beyond the unlock-level changes.
