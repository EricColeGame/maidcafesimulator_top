Maid Cafe Simulator's cast combines five named maid characters with recurring street and story characters.

Maids

The five maids are Omu, Mina, Yuki, Momo and Jennie. They join as the cafe grows; Yuki, Jennie and Momo join during the story after the demo-progress point ([launch post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067928563)).

NPC roles confirmed in patch notes

- Maidchelin judge: appears for inspections, and conversations with the judge are marked as main story with an icon and long-distance marker. You cannot sleep until you talk to them after results (v1.20).
- Regular otaku: shows up early with money tips if your balance drops below $10, and tells you about "Doki," the local currency (v1.20, v1.21).
- Deliveroid: appears next to the vending machine in front of the shop during business hours with tips about Maidroids (v1.21).
- Puppy: notices and praises your cafe level, Maidchelin stars, recipes learned and special bonds (v1.21).

Notes

The store mentions 10 customer personalities and two types of troublemakers. Romance routes are not confirmed in the official notes checked here; the store describes bond stories and "wishes," not romance endings.
