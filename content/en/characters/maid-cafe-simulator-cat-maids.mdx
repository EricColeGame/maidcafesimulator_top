## Management advice without invented numbers

Because no recipe list, price table, or maid stat sheet is published, any "optimal build" you read is guesswork dressed as fact. What you can do is reason from the systems the page describes.

First, treat stock as a deadline problem. Ordering by phone through a courier implies a gap between request and delivery. Overordering ties up money. Underordering ends service early. Watch how long deliveries actually take in your save and build a buffer.

Second, separate speed from accuracy in your own head. Scanning receipts and counting change is a distinct skill from mixing drinks. If you are failing, figure out which half is costing you.

Third, remember that three Maidchelin stars is described as an aim, not a guaranteed outcome. The page does not state the criteria, so nobody outside the developers can tell you the exact threshold.
