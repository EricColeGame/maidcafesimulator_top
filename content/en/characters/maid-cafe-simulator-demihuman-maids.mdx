In Maid Cafe Simulator the maids are kemonomimi, meaning they have animal ears and tails. The store describes them as "five charming kemonomimi maids" and the story premise involves a kemonomimi girl turning up at the final hiring interview ([Steam store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/?l=english)).

Confirmed details

- Ears and tails appear on costumes and were refined for Momo's and Yuki's exclusive outfits in v1.21.12 ([hotfix](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847286114047748)).
- Maids are recruited through the story, not a random hiring pool: Yuki, Jennie and Momo join as the narrative reaches them ([launch post](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846018067928563)).
- Customisation includes 18 outfits plus 15 DLC outfits, and the demo offers 10 summer and winter uniforms (store; hotfix).

Not documented

Race bonuses, demihuman stat differences and random recruitment systems are not described in the official notes checked here. Check the in-game maid stats screen for ability details.
