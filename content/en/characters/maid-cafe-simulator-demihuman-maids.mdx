Maid Cafe Simulator arrived on Steam on 7 October 2026 from developers Accelix and SCON, and it asks a simple question: what happens when someone hands you a rundown cafe and expects you to turn it into a three-star destination? According to the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/), the answer involves hiring maids, mixing drinks, scanning receipts, counting change, and getting to know five employees who are each hiding something. ## What Maid Cafe Simulator Is, According to Its Store Page

The official description frames the game as a first-person cafe management simulator. You inherit a rundown cafe, and a kemonomimi girl shows up and changes everything. From there, the advertised loop is hands-on rather than menu-driven: mixing drinks, serving customers, scanning receipts, and counting change. Ingredient restocking happens by phone, with a Maidroid courier handling deliveries.

That combination matters for expectations. This is not a spreadsheet tycoon game where you set prices and watch numbers move. The store description points to physical, first-person tasks you perform yourself, which means your speed and accuracy at the counter are part of the experience. The long-term goal named on the page is earning three Maidchelin stars, a parody of restaurant star ratings that gives the management side a clear finish line.

## The Five Maids and Their Secrets

The store description states that the cafe has five maids who each have a secret. That is the full extent of what the official page commits to. It does not name the maids, describe their personalities, list their stats, or explain how their secrets are revealed.

Because of that, anything you read elsewhere claiming to know a specific maid's backstory, preferred gifts, or stat growth should be treated as a third-party guide observation rather than developer documentation. Community write-ups on aggregator sites are secondary sources by nature. If you want to confirm how secrets work, the reliable path is to play and watch how dialogue and events change over time, or check the official Steam page and the developers' own channels for updates.

What the premise does imply is a design priority: the five maids are characters, not just labor units. A management sim that advertises secrets is usually signaling that relationship progress and cafe progress are connected, so paying attention to conversations is likely as useful as optimizing your drink routine.

## Reaching Three Maidchelin Stars

Three Maidchelin stars is the stated end goal, and it is the clearest signal that the game has a progression arc rather than an endless sandbox. A reasonable, generalized strategy is to treat each service day as practice toward a repeatable standard: consistent drink preparation, clean transactions, and enough stocked ingredients that you never turn a customer away. If the game tracks quality, consistency likely matters more than occasional perfect performance. None of this guarantees three stars, and the specific thresholds are something only the game itself can confirm.
