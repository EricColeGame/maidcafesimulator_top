## What Counts as a Demihuman Maid?

"Demihuman" is the umbrella term the game uses for staff with humanoid bodies and animal or fantasy traits — ears, tails, wings, horns, scales, or a mix. Fans of the genre often call these characters *kemonomimi* or simply "animal-eared maids," and community reports suggest the roster leans heavily into that aesthetic.

What separates them mechanically is usually three things:

- **Visible trait tags** that affect how certain customer types react to them
- **A signature quirk** — a small behavioral trait that triggers during shifts
- **Affinity pacing** that differs from human maids, often rewarding themed gifts or specific shift pairings

None of this is cosmetic-only. Player experience consistently points to trait tags influencing tip multipliers, so a cat-eared maid and a wolf-eared maid serving the same table can produce noticeably different results.

## Stats, Traits, and Affinity Explained

Three systems drive how a demihuman maid performs, and they interact more than most new players expect.

### Core Stats

Service governs order accuracy and speed. Charisma drives customer mood and tip size. Stamina determines how long a maid stays effective before fatigue penalties kick in. Community guides generally agree that Charisma has the highest ceiling for raw income, but only when Service is high enough to prevent order errors — mistakes cancel out the charisma bonus almost entirely.

### Trait Tags

Trait tags are the demihuman-specific layer. A customer with a "loves animals" tag seated with an animal-eared maid gets a mood boost. A customer with a "prefers quiet" tag seated with a chatty bird-type maid takes a mood hit. Matching tags is the single highest-return habit in the game.

### Affinity

Affinity grows through repeated successful shifts, themed gifts, and pairing maids with compatible coworkers. Higher affinity typically unlocks better quirk behavior and, in many cases, additional dialogue scenes. Players who rush affinity on two or three favorites usually outperform those who spread attention evenly across the whole roster.

| Priority | System | Why It Matters | When to Invest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trait tag matching | Direct mood and tip modifiers | From the first shift |
| 2 | Affinity | Unlocks quirks and story scenes | Early, on 2–3 core maids |
| 3 | Charisma | Raises tip ceiling | After Service is stable |
| 4 | Service | Prevents order errors | Steady, ongoing |
| 5 | Stamina | Reduces fatigue penalties | Before long events |

| Themed event nights | Cat | Dragon (clashes with playful tone) |

Beyond pairing, three habits consistently show up in successful runs:

- **Rotate before fatigue, not after.** A tired maid loses more value in ten minutes than a fresh one gains in thirty.
- **Keep one flexible archetype benched.** Slime and Spirit types exist for exactly this reason.
- **Re-match after every menu change.** New menu items shift which trait tags customers value.

## Building a Balanced Roster

Balance beats min-maxing in most playthroughs. A roster of two high-Charisma specialists, two high-Stamina workhorses, and one flexible fill-in covers nearly every situation the game throws at you. If you are chasing a specific ending or achievement, check what the requirement actually asks for — several routes care about archetype diversity rather than raw stats.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which demihuman maid is best for beginners in Maid Cafe Simulator?**

Wolf and Dog types are the most forgiving starting pick. Their high Stamina and solid Service mean fewer fatigue penalties and fewer order errors while you learn the floor management rhythm. Add a Fox type later once your Service stats are stable enough to support a high-Charisma, low-Stamina specialist.

**Do demihuman maids have different affinity requirements than human maids?**

Community reports suggest the general loop is the same — successful shifts, gifts, and compatible pairings — but demihuman maids tend to respond more strongly to themed gifts that match their trait tag. Giving a cat-type maid a generic gift usually produces a smaller affinity gain than a matching themed item.

**Can I recruit every demihuman maid in one playthrough?**

Most players report that roster slots and event timing make a full collection difficult in a single run. Prioritize the archetypes that fit your serving strategy, and leave flex slots open for limited-time event characters. A second playthrough focused on collection is usually the more efficient route.

**How important are trait tags compared with raw stats?**

For most players, trait tags matter more. A mid-tier maid with perfectly matched tags will typically out-earn a high-stat maid seated with mismatched customers, because mood modifiers compound across an entire shift.
