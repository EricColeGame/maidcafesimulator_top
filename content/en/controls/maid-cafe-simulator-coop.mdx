Maid Cafe Simulator has no confirmed co-op mode. The official Steam listing checked on 31 October 2026 lists the game as Single-player. It does not list co-op, online multiplayer or invites.

The maids you work with are in-game characters, not player-controlled partners. The store describes taking orders, making and serving drinks, scanning receipts and counting change, and says you share the workload with your maids when orders pile up. That is task sharing with in-game staff, not a second player joining your save.

The official announcements checked here do not describe co-op matchmaking or an invite system. To verify, check the Steam store listing's player-count tags and the developer's Steam announcements.
