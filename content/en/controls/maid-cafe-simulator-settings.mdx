The official notes checked here mention a few settings and one sound fix, but they do not publish a full settings menu walkthrough or list of sliders.

Confirmed settings-related items

- Mode selection: when starting a new game you choose Normal Mode or Story Mode. In Story Mode, cafe XP, Bond and income are doubled and most cafe-running challenge is removed. The setting is saved per save file and appears on the load screen and pause menu, and cannot be changed after choosing (v1.21).
- Sound: the store lists "Custom Volume Controls" as a feature. v1.20 fixed the puppy's and judge's wish voice lines ignoring volume settings; they now follow the sound-effects volume. That confirms at least a sound-effects category separate from other audio. An earlier demo update (v1.16.22) also added graphics quality presets and an option to turn off screen shake, though the official notes do not describe those current menus.
- Bug reporting: F8 or the Report Bug option in the ESC menu.

Not documented in these sources: the full list of sliders, keybind remapping, current graphics presets or a language submenu. The store lists eight supported languages, with full audio marked for Korean, but does not describe a settings submenu. For exact options, check the in-game settings menu.
