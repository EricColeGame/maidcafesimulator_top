Maid Cafe Simulator appeared on Steam on 7 October 2026, developed by Accelix and SCON. It is a first-person cafe management game: you inherit a rundown cafe, staff it with maids, mix drinks, serve customers, handle the register, and work toward three Maidchelin stars. Five maids each carry a secret, and somewhere in the mix a kemonomimi girl changes everything.

That is a lot of premise in a short store description. It is also almost all the official information that exists. If you are the kind of player who likes to read a recipe spreadsheet before buying a cooking game, this article is about what you can and cannot know in advance — and how to check the rest yourself inside the game.

## The advertised loop, in order

According to the official Steam listing for [Maid Cafe Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/), the game advertises the following activities:

- **Inheriting a rundown cafe.** You start with a business that needs work, not a finished venue.
- **Hiring maids.** Staffing is part of the management layer, not a fixed cast.
- **Mixing drinks.** Drink preparation is a hands-on task rather than a menu click.
- **Serving customers.** First-person service is the core interaction.
- **Scanning receipts and counting change.** The register is modeled at the transaction level, meaning money handling is part of the skill loop rather than automatic.
- **Ordering ingredients by phone, delivered by a Maidroid courier.** Restocking is a distinct step with its own interface and character.
- **Aiming for three Maidchelin stars.** This is the stated progression goal, framed as an achievement to build toward rather than a starting condition.
- **Five maids, each with a secret.** The narrative hook is character-based and distributed across the roster rather than concentrated in one storyline.

Note what is absent: no recipe list, no prices, no maid stat tables, no endings, no code system. The store page simply does not publish them. Any guide that presents those as settled facts is either working from playtime observation or guessing.

## How to verify anything yourself

The fastest verification method is the game itself, and it costs nothing extra beyond the purchase. Load a save, deliberately fail an order, and watch what the interface reports. The register step is a good place to start because scanning receipts and counting change are explicitly advertised — meaning the game itself is the authoritative reference for how those steps behave.

For anything about the overall shape of the game rather than moment-to-moment mechanics, the [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/) is the only source that speaks for the developers. Check the store description, the screenshots, and the community hub discussions separately; user posts in the hub are community content, not official statements, even when they are accurate.

## Practical management reasoning

Without documented numbers, the useful advice is structural rather than numerical.

**Protect the register step.** Counting change is advertised as a hands-on task. In practice, that means register errors are a likely source of lost money or unhappy customers, and it is the one part of the loop where rushing has an obvious cost. Slow down there before you slow down anywhere else.

**Treat ordering as a pacing decision.** Ingredient ordering happens by phone with a Maidroid courier. Whenever a game separates restocking from serving, the interesting question is timing: order before you run out, not after. You can only learn your own consumption rate by playing a few shifts and watching what sells.

**Let the maids' secrets set your priorities.** The premise promises five characters with hidden stories. If narrative is what you came for, spreading attention across the roster is the safe assumption; if it is not, you can likely optimize purely for the star rating. The game does not advertise a penalty for either approach, and no source confirms one exists.

**Use the star goal as a direction, not a checklist.** "Three Maidchelin stars" tells you there is a ceiling to reach. It does not tell you the criteria, and no official source publishes them. Expect to infer the requirements from in-game feedback.

## A note on expectations

Maid Cafe Simulator is a first-person simulator with a management layer and a character layer. That combination usually means the register, the drink mixing, and the ingredient runs are where the challenge lives, while the maid stories are where the reward lives. Nothing in the official material contradicts that reading, but nothing confirms the specific balance either.

What the official material does confirm is narrow and reliable: two developers, a 7 October 2026 release, a rundown cafe, five maids with secrets, drink mixing, serving, receipt scanning, change counting, phone-based ingredient ordering via a Maidroid courier, and three Maidchelin stars to chase. Everything detail-level beyond that is something you verify in-game or on the store page.
