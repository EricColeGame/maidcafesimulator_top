The official notes checked here confirm only a handful of inputs; they do not include a full keyboard and mouse table. Here is what is documented:

- F8: report a bug from inside the game (multiple patch notes).
- ESC: open the menu; the ESC menu also contains a Report Bug option.
- ESC while placing furniture: works like R, sending the furniture back to its box and canceling placement (v1.20).
- R: the key that mirrors ESC for canceling furniture placement (v1.20).
- Hold Q: throw boxes, as taught when you receive Mina's box-throwing wish (v1.21).

The notes also mention number keys for cameras during filming-night performances (the current camera's number is highlighted, v1.21), but that is specific to that mode, not a general control.

Not documented in these sources: movement keys, interact key, menu hotkeys and mouse bindings. For those, check the in-game key help or settings menu.
