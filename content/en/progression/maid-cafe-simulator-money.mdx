In Maid Cafe Simulator, starting money is $150, raised from $100 in v1.20. Rich customers now carry $100-220, so pricier drinks sell better. Story Mode doubles income, but only for a new game started in that mode.

**Confirmed facts**
- Starting money raised from $100 to $150 (v1.20, October 16, 2026).
- Rich customers now carry $100-220 (v1.20).
- If money drops below $10, the regular otaku on the street appears early with money-making tips (v1.20).
- The register can no longer be sold, closing one early way to lose money (v1.20).
- Story Mode doubles cafe XP, Bond and income, and removes most of the challenge. It is chosen at new game, saved per file, and cannot be changed later; existing saves stay on Normal Mode (v1.21, October 23, 2026).
- The mode is visible on the load screen and pause menu (v1.21).

**Unknowns**
No official money-making exploit or full customer payment formula is documented in the sources checked here. Treat any claimed exploit as unverified.
