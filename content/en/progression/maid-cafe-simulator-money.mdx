## How to Approach Your First Shifts

Because recipes and prices are not documented in advance, your first sessions are best spent learning the game's own feedback. General practice for this kind of simulator:

- Read every on-screen prompt during your first service. The game teaches mixing and scanning in context rather than through a manual.
- Complete each transaction fully, including scanning and change, before moving on. Partial service tends to create a queue you cannot recover from.
- Watch which drinks customers order most and keep those ingredients stocked before your shift, not during it.
- Talk to the maids between shifts. The store page confirms each has a secret, so relationship progress is likely tied to the story rather than to a stat screen you can min-max.

None of this guarantees stars. It is simply the order of operations that a first-person service game rewards.
