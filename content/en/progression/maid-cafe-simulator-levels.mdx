Cafe levels run to 40 and maid levels to 15, according to the launch announcement and store listing. The v1.21 update (October 23, 2026) reduced the XP needed for Cafe Lv6-10 by about 30%. That is the only XP quantification in the notes checked here; no XP table or per-level totals are given, so exact XP figures are not listed.

Known milestone levels (v1.21 unless noted)

- Cafe Lv6: Espresso recipe buyable ($6).
- Cafe Lv7: 1-star Maidchelin stage; Latte recipe in shop; "two stations for higher-tier recipes" tip appears at Lv6-7.
- Cafe Lv10: Triple Fruit recipe buyable ($105, v1.20).
- Cafe Lv11: 2-star Maidchelin inspection and Jennie/Momo joining story.
- Lv6-10: XP requirement reduced about 30%.

Things to know

- Bond stories unlock after Jennie and Momo join, spread from Cafe Lv13 to Lv39, per v1.20.
- Venue and stage unlocks are tied to expansions and wishes (the first hall expansion is guided through Mina's wish), not only to levels.
- For actual in-game XP numbers, check your save's progression screen.
