As of the v1.21 update (October 23, 2026), the 1-star Maidchelin stage needs Cafe Lv7 (down from Lv8), and the Espresso recipe can be bought in the shop from Lv6 for $6. These are unlocks, not achievement requirements: they gate the menu you need for the star. The 2-star inspection and the Jennie and Momo joining story need Cafe Lv11 (was Lv12 in v1.20); v1.20 had already set the level at Lv12, down from Lv13.

What unlocks at each point

- Espresso: buyable at Cafe Lv6 ($6). Before v1.21 it came only from Mina's wish, which could block the 1-star menu.
- Latte: buyable in the shop from Cafe Lv7, per the v1.21 notes.
- 1-star Maidchelin stage: Cafe Lv7 after v1.21.
- 2-star Maidchelin inspection and Jennie/Momo joining: Cafe Lv11 after v1.21.
- Triple Fruit recipe: buyable from Cafe Lv10 ($105), per v1.20.

Story triggers

Yuki's joining story no longer plays automatically at sleep; it starts when you talk to Yuki in front of the shop the next morning. Episode 5 also starts in the morning before opening. Jennie's Bond Story 3 unlocks at night after closing (changed in v1.21).

Achievements are separate from progression unlocks. The store lists 23 achievements total (20 regular, 3 hidden), including "First Sale," "Exact Change Pro," "Signature Menu" and "Nothing Left to Learn." The exact unlock conditions for each are not in the sources checked here and remain unconfirmed.
