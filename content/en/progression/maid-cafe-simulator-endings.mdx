The Steam store lists 13 main story chapters and 36 bond stories. The launch announcement says the main story continues past the demo and closes on an ending sequence and credits set to a vocal version of the theme. That is the ending structure the official sources describe.

What is confirmed

- The main story runs to an ending sequence with credits.
- v1.20 includes a fix for the ending replay not closing after the credits, which confirms an ending replay exists.
- Bond stories open up "to the end" for all five maids, per the launch announcement.

What is not documented here

The official notes checked here do not describe multiple ending branches, a true ending, a solo or single-maid ending, or a failure ending. An ending replay feature only confirms you can replay the ending; it does not show branching endings exist. The sources checked do not include a list of endings.

For story and replay conditions, check the in-game story replay on the laptop, which shows unlock conditions for stories, and watch official announcements. Bond story unlock conditions were changed in v1.20 and hint text was corrected to match them.
