Start a new game on at least v1.21, then decide between Normal Mode and Story Mode as soon as you begin: the choice is saved per file and cannot be changed later. Existing saves keep running in Normal Mode; Story Mode requires a new game. If you want a relaxed, story-first run, Story Mode doubles cafe XP, Bond and income and removes most of the cafe challenge. Otherwise stay on Normal Mode.

For day-to-day service, the official store description frames the loop as mixing drinks, serving customers, scanning receipts and counting out change. If ingredients run low, you order on your phone and a Maidroid courier delivers them; when orders pile up, share the work with your maids. My suggestion is to restock before a rush rather than mid-rush, and not to sell the register, which the v1.20 notes say can no longer be sold because it could strand players.

Exact wages, hire costs and payout formulas are not given in the official notes checked here, so check the in-game menus rather than assuming figures. Espresso can be bought from the shop at Cafe Lv6 for $6, and the Latte recipe from Lv7 (v1.21).
