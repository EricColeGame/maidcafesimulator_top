## What Maid Cafe Simulator Is, Per the Official Store Page

Maid Cafe Simulator is a first-person cafe management sim developed by Accelix and SCON and released on Steam on 7 October 2026. According to the store description, you inherit a rundown cafe. A kemonomimi girl arrives and changes everything. From there the game becomes a maid cafe you run: you hire maids, mix drinks, serve customers, scan receipts, count change, and order ingredients by phone from a Maidroid courier. The long-term goal is three Maidchelin stars.

The page also states there are five maids, and that each one has a secret. That is the entire documented premise. Notably, the store description does not publish a recipe list, item prices, maid stat tables, or a code system. If you see those things claimed elsewhere, treat them as third-party observations or speculation until you can confirm them in your own copy of the game.

## Practical Reasoning About the Management Loop

The advertised loop has a clear shape: money in, ingredients out, reputation up. Scanning receipts and counting change are the money-in steps. Ordering ingredients by phone is the money-out step. Mixing drinks and serving customers sit in the middle. Three Maidchelin stars is the stated long-term target.

General management practice, framed as reasoning rather than documented data:

- Treat the receipt-and-change steps as your accuracy check. In any cafe sim, small errors compound over a shift, so slow down on those interactions before optimizing speed anywhere else.
- Keep ingredient orders ahead of demand rather than reacting after you run out. The store page confirms phone ordering, not restock timers, so watch your own shelves to learn the rhythm.
- Do not assume maids are interchangeable. The page says each of the five has a secret, which suggests personal story beats are part of the design, not just labor. Investing in getting to know them is plausibly part of the intended experience, though the page does not spell out rewards.
- Aim at the three-star target gradually. The description presents it as an ambition, not a checklist, and it does not publish the criteria. Learn the criteria by playing.

None of this guarantees three Maidchelin stars. It is reasoning from the advertised systems, not a walkthrough.
