## Where the Maid Cafe Simulator Release Date Is Officially Confirmed

The most reliable source is always the game's own store listing. On PC, that usually means the [official Steam store page for Maid Cafe Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=Maid+Cafe+Simulator), where the release date field sits directly under the trailer. If that field says "Coming Soon" or "To Be Announced," it is not a delay — it simply means the developer has not committed to a public date yet.

From there, work outward through sources that are accountable for what they publish.

| Source | Reliability | What to look for |
|---|---|---|
| Official store listing | Highest | Release date field, demo availability, Early Access label |
| Developer or publisher site and social accounts | High | Devlogs, teaser trailers, press releases |
| Store wishlist or follow notifications | High | Automatic email or app alert the moment it launches |
| Established gaming media outlets | Medium-high | Confirm every claim against the official listing |
| Forums, Discord, and comment sections | Low | Community reports and rumors — treat as unverified |

A useful habit: when you see a date quoted somewhere, open the store listing in a second tab. If the two disagree, the store listing wins every time. Developers update their own pages first and rarely announce changes anywhere else simultaneously.

Community reports can still be genuinely useful. If a developer starts posting weekly screenshots, a store page suddenly gains a demo, or a Steam depot quietly updates, those are signals that a launch is getting close. Just don't build your calendar around a screenshot of a chat message.

### Early Access vs. Full Launch

Many indie sims arrive in Early Access first, then reach 1.0 later. If the store page carries an Early Access banner on the Maid Cafe Simulator release date, expect a playable but evolving game: content updates, balance changes, and a roadmap. If there's no banner, you're looking at a finished release. Either way, read the "About This Game" section carefully — that's where developers state plainly what is and isn't included yet.

### System Requirements and Performance

Check the requirements table on the listing before you buy, especially if you're on a laptop. Cafe sims are usually light, but crowd simulation and character models can push integrated graphics harder than expected. Compare your GPU and RAM against the listed minimum, not the recommended spec, to know whether you'll need to lower settings.
