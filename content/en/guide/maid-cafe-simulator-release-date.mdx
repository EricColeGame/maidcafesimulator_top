## What the Steam Page Advertises

The official description is the authority on the game's identity. According to it, Maid Cafe Simulator is built around a specific set of tasks:

- You inherit a cafe that is described as rundown, so early play is presumably about getting it into presentable shape.
- A kemonomimi girl appears and, per the store text, changes everything.
- The cafe staffs five maids, and each one has a secret.
- The long-term goal is earning three Maidchelin stars.
- Hands-on duties include mixing drinks, serving customers, scanning receipts, and counting change.
- Ingredient restocking happens by phone, with a Maidroid courier handling delivery.

Two things are notable here. First, the game is first-person, which the store framing supports and marketing materials show. Second, the register work is treated as a real mechanic rather than a background animation — scanning receipts and counting change are listed alongside mixing drinks, so expect them to demand attention during a rush.
