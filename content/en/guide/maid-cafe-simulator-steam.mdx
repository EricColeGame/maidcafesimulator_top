Maid Cafe Simulator is on Steam for Windows and macOS; Linux is not listed. The store lists it as Single-player, with Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, Family Sharing and Custom Volume Controls. The US price was $23.99 when checked on 31 October 2026, with no discount shown; regional prices and discounts vary, and a 20% launch discount ran through October 19, 2026.

A separate demo is listed on the same store page (App ID 4416560). The store description lists 13 main story chapters, 36 bond stories, five kemonomimi maids, 49 drink recipes and three drink-making station types, 18 outfits plus 15 DLC outfits, and 23 achievements (20 regular, three hidden). Supported languages are English, Japanese, Korean (with full audio), Simplified and Traditional Chinese, German, Russian and Latin American Spanish.

System requirements listed on the store. Windows minimum: Windows 11 64-bit, i5 3550 or Ryzen 5 2500X, 8 GB RAM, GTX 1050 or R9 270X, DirectX 11, 5 GB storage. Windows recommended: Windows 11 64-bit, i5 7600K or Ryzen 5 2600X, 16 GB RAM, GTX 2070 or AMD 5700 XT, 5 GB storage. macOS minimum: Apple processor (M1 or better), macOS 12.0 or newer, 8 GB RAM, 10 GB storage. macOS recommended: Apple M1 Pro or better, macOS 12.0 or newer, 8 GB RAM, 10 GB storage.

This is a feature overview from the official listing, not a hands-on review. Controller support is not listed on the store page.
