Maid Cafe Simulator is a first-person cafe management game from developers Accelix and SCON, released on Steam on 7 October 2026. You inherit a rundown cafe, hire maids, mix drinks, serve customers, handle the register, and chase the highest culinary honor in this fictional world: three Maidchelin stars. The premise is cozy on the surface and a little strange underneath, since each of the five maids is hiding a secret.

This article sticks to what the developers actually advertise, notes where community guides fill in gaps, and is honest about what nobody has documented yet. If you want the primary source, start with the official [Maid Cafe Simulator Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3232690/).

## What the developers actually advertise

The Steam store description is the authority here. It confirms these features:

- You inherit a rundown cafe and work to restore it.
- A kemonomimi girl (a character with animal ears) arrives and changes everything.
- The cafe staffs five maids, and each one has a secret.
- The long-term goal is three Maidchelin stars.
- Core tasks include mixing drinks, serving customers, scanning receipts, and counting change.
- You order ingredients by phone, and a Maidroid courier delivers them.

That is the full advertised feature set. Notice what is *not* there: no recipe list, no item prices, no maid stat tables, and no code system. Any guide claiming exact recipe ratios, menu prices, or maid stats is either working from datamined files or guessing. Treat those numbers as secondary observations, not developer documentation.

## The moment-to-moment loop, in practical terms

Based on the advertised features, a typical session likely follows a recognizable service loop:

1. Check stock and order missing ingredients by phone.
2. Wait for the Maidroid courier delivery.
3. Prepare drinks while customers arrive.
4. Serve orders before patience runs out.
5. Ring up the sale: scan the receipt, then count out change.
6. Repeat until the shift ends and you can reinvest in the cafe.

What stands out is the register step. Scanning receipts and counting change is unusual for this genre, which usually abstracts payment into a single button press. It suggests the game wants you to feel the rhythm of a real counter shift, not just the strategy layer. Because the store page does not describe how strict the change-counting is, verify it yourself in the tutorial before you assume it works like a minigame with a pass or fail state.

### A general drink-mixing approach

Since no recipe list is published, avoid memorizing ratios from third-party posts. Instead, build habits that survive any recipe system:

| Habit | Why it helps |

## Working toward three Maidchelin stars

Three Maidchelin stars is the stated end goal, but the Steam page does not publish the scoring criteria. Community guides speculate that quality, speed, and staff relationships all feed into it, but that is not confirmed by the developers.

A reasonable, non-guaranteed approach:
