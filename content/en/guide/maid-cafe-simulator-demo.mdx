The demo is a separate Steam listing (App ID 4416560) linked from the full game's store page. Per the launch announcement, the demo covers the first four story chapters and Cafe Level 10, while the full game continues with the rest of the main story, its ending, three more maids, Maidchelin inspections, festivals and the Dance and Performance Stages.

Save carry-over had a rough start. The October 13 update said demo saves were not transferring correctly and told players to start a new game. The v1.20 update (October 16) reported that the demo save issue was fixed, so demo saves can now carry over to the full game.

The newest demo change is in v1.21.12 (October 27), which adds the ability to choose from 10 summer and winter uniforms for all five maids in the demo.

The official notes checked here do not state the demo's current content cap beyond the four chapters and Cafe Level 10 described at launch, so check the demo listing for any later changes.
