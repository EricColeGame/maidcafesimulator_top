## What the Store Page Actually Confirms

The verified store description gives a clear picture of the premise. You inherit a rundown cafe. A kemonomimi girl arrives and changes everything. You build the place into a maid cafe staffed by five maids, each of whom has a secret. Your long-term goal is three Maidchelin stars.

The moment-to-moment work is described as:

- Mixing drinks
- Serving customers
- Scanning receipts
- Counting change
- Ordering ingredients by phone, with a Maidroid courier handling delivery

That is the advertised feature set. It is a first-person perspective, so expect to physically move around the cafe rather than click through menus from a top-down view.

## A Practical Approach to Your First Shifts

Because the store page does not hand you a recipe book, early play is about observation rather than memorizing a spreadsheet.

Start by learning the drink menu the game gives you in-game. Mixing drinks is a confirmed core action, so the order of ingredients and the timing of each step is something you will likely learn by doing and by watching customer reactions. Rather than chasing a copied ratio list, pay attention to whether a customer accepts or rejects what you serve; that feedback is your real tutorial.

Receipts and change are the part new players underestimate. Scanning a receipt and counting change are both explicitly listed, which suggests the game treats checkout as a hands-on task rather than an automatic transaction. Take your time at the register. Rushing the change-counting step is the kind of mistake that costs you money without teaching you anything.

## Running the Cafe as a Business

Management simulators usually reward a slow build over aggressive expansion, and the confirmed loop here supports that: you order ingredients by phone, a Maidroid courier delivers them, and you convert those ingredients into drinks served to customers.

A sensible operating rhythm:

1. Track which drinks sell and which ingredients sit unused before you reorder.
2. Keep enough stock to survive a busy stretch, but avoid tying up money in ingredients that spoil or go unused.
3. Get faster at checkout, since scanning and change-counting both consume time you could spend serving.
4. Watch how your maids perform on shift rather than assuming they are interchangeable.
