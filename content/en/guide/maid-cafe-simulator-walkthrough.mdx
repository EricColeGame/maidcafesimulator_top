On v1.21, the 1-star Maidchelin stage needs Cafe Lv7 (reduced from Lv8). Buy the Espresso recipe in the shop from Cafe Lv6 for $6, and the Latte recipe from Lv7; the 1-star menu guide is now split into "Reach Cafe Lv7" and "Buy the Espresso, Steamed Milk and Latte recipes." The 2-star Maidchelin inspection, together with Jennie and Momo's joining story, needs Cafe Lv11 (down from Lv12).

Story triggers changed: Yuki's joining story no longer plays when you go to sleep. It starts the next morning when you talk to Yuki in front of the shop. Episode 5 also begins in the morning before opening. "Wait until evening" is now "Close the cafe for today." After a Maidchelin inspection, wait for the results, then close the cafe and meet the judge in front of the shop; you cannot sleep until you have talked to them. Judge conversations are marked as main story. The v1.21 fix also corrects a case where the judge could disappear after reloading if you quit without saving after the results conversation.

My suggestion: follow the in-game menu guide step by step and buy recipes as soon as their level unlocks. The earlier v1.20 notes also specify 16 recipes and Fruit Blend for the 2-star inspection; v1.21 lowers the level gate to Lv11. Follow the quest checklist as well as the level gate.
