This is a source-based feature overview rather than a hands-on review, so it does not give a score or a played verdict.

**What the game is.** Maid Cafe Simulator is a first-person cafe management sim from Accelix and SCON, published by Accelix on Steam for Windows and Mac. The Steam store displays a release date of 7 October 2026. Its US price was $23.99 USD when checked on 31 October 2026; regional prices and discounts vary. The premise is a rundown cafe you inherit, five kemonomimi maids with their own secrets, and a goal of three Maidchelin stars.

**Scope listed on the store page.** 13 main story chapters and 36 bond stories; 25 text messages per maid; 10 customer personalities and two types of troublemakers; 49 drink recipes across three drink-making station types; 18 outfits plus 15 DLC outfits; full Korean voice acting for the main story and bond stories; 20 regular and three hidden achievements (23 total). The launch announcement adds cafe levels up to 40, maid levels to 15, 13 rotating festivals, and 100 late-night text stories.

**Systems named in announcements.** A Story Mode added in v1.21 doubles cafe XP, Bond and income and removes most of the challenge; the mode is chosen per save and cannot be changed. A Dance Stage and a filming-night Performance Stage give maids performances. A Maidchelin inspector appears when requirements are met.

**Demo for assessment.** A demo is listed on Steam (app 4416560). The developers stated it covers the first four story chapters and Cafe Level 10. The v1.21.12 hotfix added 10 summer and winter uniforms for all five maids in the demo. Demo save carry-over to the full game was broken at launch and fixed in the v1.20 update.

No hands-on performance or review scores are claimed here, because the official sources checked do not include them.
