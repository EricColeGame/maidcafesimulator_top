The Maid Cafe Simulator menu is a drink menu: the Steam store listing advertises 49 drink recipes across three types of drink-making station. No food main-dish lineup is described on the menu, so a burger, curry or omurice menu is not confirmed. A Cooking Fest post from launch said "48 menu items" (October 13, 2026); the current store listing states 49 drink recipes, so treat 49 as the current figure.

**Confirmed facts**
- 49 drink recipes and three station types (blender, brewer, shaker) are listed on the official store page.
- Pro blenders and pro brewers make two of the same drink from one serving of ingredients, per the v1.20 notes (October 16, 2026).
- Higher-tier recipes need two stations, per the in-game tip added in v1.21 (October 23, 2026).
- The station recipe selection screen shows sale prices, and festival menu items show their raised prices (v1.21).
- Documented recipe unlocks and prices: Espresso can be bought from Cafe Lv6 for $6 (v1.21); Triple Fruit from Lv10 for $105 (v1.20); Latte from Lv7 (v1.21).

**Unknowns**
Full recipe names, ingredient quantities and complete shop prices are not in the official sources checked here. Check the in-game recipe book or a later patch note for the exact values.
