Food in Maid Cafe Simulator is documented mainly as maid gifts and feeding, not as cafe menu mains. The official store menu is drinks (49 recipes), so a curry, omurice or other main-dish menu is not confirmed. Do not assume food dishes are sellable just because the game is a cafe sim.

**Confirmed facts**
- Maids can gift cooking stations as bond deepens, and existing food gifts can be received at more bond levels (v1.21, October 23, 2026).
- Yuki's feeding wish now accepts any food, replacing a specific-food requirement (v1.20, October 16, 2026).
- Feeding a maid during a conversation shows her energy bar rise with bond (v1.20).
- Feeding reactions now have motions and expressions matching each maid's personality; Jennie now likes cola too (v1.21).

**Unknowns**
There is no documented cafe food main menu, food recipe list or food prices in the official sources checked here. Check in-game menus or later patch notes for any food lineup.
