Drinks in Maid Cafe Simulator are made at three station types: blender, brewer and shaker. The official store lists 49 drink recipes, and the v1.20 update made the brewer and blender minigames show the recipe and remaining count in the top left, matching the shaker.

**Confirmed facts**
- Stations without a registered recipe show "Register recipe" instead of "Cook" (v1.21, October 23, 2026).
- While a maid is cooking, the recipe icon's border shows progress (v1.21).
- Pro blenders and pro brewers make two of the same drink from one serving of ingredients (v1.20, October 16, 2026).
- The "Use two stations for higher-tier recipes" tip appears at Lv6-7 (v1.21).
- Maids now take cooking stations for the pricier items on sale first (v1.20).
- Recipe shop prices are partly documented: Espresso from Cafe Lv6 at $6, Latte from Lv7 (v1.21); Triple Fruit from Lv10 at $105 (v1.20).

**Unknowns**
The full recipe list, exact ingredient amounts and every recipe price are not in the official sources checked here. Verify current values in game after patches.
