Core cafe items in Maid Cafe Simulator include the phone, ingredient shelf, box shelf, cooking stations and the register. The phone lets you order ingredients and have a Maidroid courier deliver them to your door, per the official store page.

**Confirmed facts**
- The register can no longer be sold, because selling it early left players unable to buy it back (v1.20, October 16, 2026).
- When maids tidy boxes, they fill box shelves first; with no room, contents go onto the ingredient shelf (v1.21, October 23, 2026).
- Stations without a registered recipe show "Register recipe" instead of "Cook" (v1.21).
- Pro blenders and pro brewers yield two of the same drink per serving (v1.20).
- A Deliveroid appears next to the vending machine in front of the shop during business hours, with Maidroid tips (v1.21).
- Bottom-slot ingredient shelf usability and box-shelf aim issues were fixed (v1.20).

**Unknowns**
Individual item prices and shop unlock levels are not in the sources checked here, so none are listed.
