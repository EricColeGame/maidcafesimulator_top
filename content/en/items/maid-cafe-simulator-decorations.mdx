Decorations in Maid Cafe Simulator mean furniture and equipment placed in the cafe. Pressing ESC while placing furniture now works like R: it returns the item to its box and cancels placement (v1.20). The Dance Stage and game table are unlocked through tasks or wishes, not simply bought at the start.

**Confirmed facts**
- ESC during furniture placement returns the item and cancels placement, same as R (v1.20, October 16, 2026).
- The Play and Perform slots on the chalkboard can only be checked once you have built a game table or the Dance Stage (v1.20).
- Mina's game table wish is a new wish from v1.21 (October 23, 2026).
- Furniture gifts are added to a maid's gift list once that furniture's shop level is reached (v1.21).
- Turning off indoor lights is saved even after expanding or loading, and dims the room slightly instead of pitch darkness (v1.21).

**Practical suggestion, not a stat bonus**
Group related stations and shelves near the counter to reduce walking. The notes also say customer bugs were fixed where furniture placement blocked shop entry, so if customers stop coming in, recheck placement.

**Unknowns**
No official list of decoration items with stat effects is in the sources checked here. Do not claim placement gives bonuses.
