Yes, community-made Maid Cafe Simulator mods have author pages on Nexus. These are unofficial projects; the checked Steam listing does not advertise Steam Workshop or an official modding tool.

## Examples with author sources

- [SortIT by Remikje](https://www.nexusmods.com/maidcafesimulator/mods/2) organizes storage by shelf row, remembers assigned item types and lets the player replace an empty row's assignment. Its author also describes drink-shelf sorting.
- [Thai localization by Arai / MisakiArai](https://www.nexusmods.com/maidcafesimulator/mods/1?tab=description) translates the game into Thai. It is a fan translation, not a developer language pack.

## SortIT installation, according to its author

1. Install MelonLoader with IL2CPP support, selecting MaidCafeSimulator.exe. The author specifies v0.6.x or newer.
2. Run the game once to create the loader folders, then close it.
3. Place SortIT.dll in the game's Mods folder and relaunch. The author says the MelonLoader console confirms loading.

These are the author's Windows instructions, not a procedure tested by this wiki. The Thai mod has its own dependency and Mods/Plugins folder instructions; use its page rather than assuming all mods install identically.

## Check compatibility after patches

SortIT's page shows an update on 17 October 2026, preceding the game's v1.21 and v1.21.12 updates. Compatibility with those newer builds has not been established here, nor has macOS installation. Check the author's current requirements and posts for your installed version.
