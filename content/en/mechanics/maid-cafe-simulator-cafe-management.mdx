Cafe management in Maid Cafe Simulator is first-person and hands-on: take orders, make and serve drinks, scan receipts, count out change, and order ingredients from your phone for a Maidroid courier to deliver. You can share work with maids when orders pile up. The store describes 10 customer personalities and two types of troublemakers; troublemakers can be escorted outside.

**Chalkboard.** The chalkboard holds tasks such as Momo's wish step to "Double-check Momo's Cook task on the Chalkboard", which v1.20.38 fixed. Play and Perform slots on the chalkboard can only be checked once a game table or Dance Stage is built (v1.20).

**Shelves and boxes.** When tidying boxes on the floor, maids now fill box shelves first, and if there is no room, empty the ingredients onto the ingredient shelf (v1.21). A "How to use shelves" help tip was added in v1.21. v1.20 fixed the bottom slot of the ingredient shelf being unusable.

**Lost and found and receipts.** Items that fall out of the map now return to the lost and found area (v1.20). Receipts are scanned as part of the register workflow; the "Exact Change Pro" achievement exists for change handling. The register can no longer be sold since v1.20.

Maid wages, staff costs and full economy formulas are not given in the official notes checked here; check in-game menus and patch notes for current figures.

**Practical suggestion.** Check shelf space before ordering ingredients and clear delivery boxes before opening. The v1.21 box-sorting change makes the box shelf the first destination for maids tidying boxes.
