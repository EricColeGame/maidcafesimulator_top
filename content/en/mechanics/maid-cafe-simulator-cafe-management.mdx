The premise is simple to state and surprisingly broad in scope: you inherit a rundown cafe, a kemonomimi girl changes everything, and you hire five maids who each have a secret. Your long-term goal is three Maidchelin stars. Getting there means mixing drinks, serving customers, scanning receipts, counting change, and ordering ingredients by phone from a Maidroid courier.

## What the Official Store Page Advertises
