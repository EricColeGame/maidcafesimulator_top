The store page lists 49 drink recipes and three types of drink-making station. That count changed over time: an earlier Cooking Fest announcement cited 48 menu items, and the store now says 49, so treat the store figure as current. Full recipe lists are not published in the official texts checked here, so no complete list is given below.

**Stations.** The store names three drink-making station types. Updates also mention Blenders, Brewers and Shakers, and v1.20 specifically says the Pro Blender and Pro Brewer each make two of the same drink from one serving of ingredients. A v1.21 help tip says using two stations is the route to higher-tier recipes.

**Prices confirmed in patch notes (v1.21 values override earlier ones):**
- Espresso: $6, buyable in the shop from Cafe Lv6 since v1.21. Before that it was only available through Mina's wish, which could block the 1-star menu.
- Latte: buyable from Cafe Lv7 since v1.21.
- Triple Fruit: $105, buyable in the shop from Cafe Lv10 since v1.20.

**Practical suggestion.** v1.21 splits the 1-star menu guide into "Reach Cafe Lv7" and "Buy the Espresso, Steamed Milk and Latte recipes", so buying those shop recipes early removes a known earlier blocker.

A full list of all 49 recipes, their ingredients and sale prices is not given in the official notes checked here. Check the in-game recipe book or station selection screen, which v1.21 now shows sale prices on.
