Maid Cafe Simulator has five named maids, and they join through story progression rather than random hiring. The official notes checked here do not describe a recruitment pool or randomised applicants.

**Jennie and Momo.** These two join through the 2-star Maidchelin stage. v1.20 set that stage to Cafe Lv12 and 16 recipes, with Fruit Blend required for the 2-star inspection. v1.21 moved it again to Cafe Lv11; the old requirement was Cafe Lv12 in v1.20 and Lv13 before that. The 1-star Maidchelin stage now needs Cafe Lv7 in v1.21 (was Lv8).

**Yuki.** Since v1.21, Yuki's joining story no longer plays automatically when you go to sleep. It starts when you talk to Yuki in front of the shop the next morning. Episode 5 also begins in the morning before opening.

**Hall slots per expansion stage.** v1.21 notes that hall expansion descriptions now show how many maids can work at each stage: 3, 4 or 5. A full work schedule now shows whether expanding the hall adds slots. The first hall expansion is guided through Mina's wish rather than a main quest (v1.21).

**Not documented.** Wages, hiring costs, and specific maid traits are not given in the sources checked here. A Jennie wish to "check her traits" is mentioned, but its contents are not.
