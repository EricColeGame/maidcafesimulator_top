Cafe upgrades in Maid Cafe Simulator are split between building expansions and equipment, and the newest patch changes several older requirements. Since v1.21, the kitchen expansion is no longer required for Episodes 5 and 7; expanding the hall is enough ([v1.21](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1847286114035125)).

Confirmed changes

- Display shelf expansions 2 and 3 now unlock at Cafe Lv3 and Lv5, moved earlier so early money is not tied up ([v1.20](https://steamstore-a.akamaihd.net/news/externalpost/steam_community_announcements/1846651706680712)).
- The first hall expansion is now guided through Mina's wish rather than a main quest (v1.21).
- Expansion descriptions now show how many maids can work at each stage: 3, 4 or 5 (v1.21).
- The pro blender and pro brewer descriptions now state they make two of the same drink from one serving of ingredients (v1.20).

Practical suggestions

If shelf space is your bottleneck, check the display shelf expansions unlocked at Lv3 and Lv5 before spending on unrelated equipment. When planning hall size, use the 3/4/5 maid figure shown in the Expansion app rather than guessing.

Not documented

Exact construction costs and full upgrade tiers are not given in the official notes checked here. Check the in-game Expansion app for current prices.
