Only limited maid-progression details are confirmed in the official notes checked here; no official stat name or skill tree list is given, so none are listed.

**Failed tasks.** In v1.21, energy lost when failing Perform or Play tasks rose from 1 to 5, because failing barely cost energy before. Play and Perform slots require a game table or Dance Stage first (v1.20).

**Bond gains (v1.21 values override earlier ones):**
- Reading a maid's text message to the end gives +10 Bond, for messages read after the update.
- Watching a bond story to the end gives +50 Bond.
- The daily cap from petting was lowered from 40 to 20 per maid, in exchange for the above.

**Gifts.** Maids can gift cooking stations; as bond deepens, they gift higher-tier Blenders, Brewers and Shakers. Furniture gifts appear once that furniture's shop level is reached. A "Gift on its way!" notification appears for delivery gifts. The store lists 18 outfits plus 15 DLC outfits.

**Not documented.** The official notes checked here do not name individual skill stats, upgrade costs, or tier ratings. v1.21 adds a voice reaction when you open or upgrade a maid's stats.
