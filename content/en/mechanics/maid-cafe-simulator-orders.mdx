Order handling in Maid Cafe Simulator is hands-on: mix drinks, serve customers, scan receipts, and count out change. When orders pile up, maids can share the workload. Ingredients are ordered on your phone and delivered by a Maidroid courier.

**Stations and registered recipes.** Since v1.21, stations without a registered recipe show "Register recipe" instead of "Cook". The wish step "Register a recipe at a Cook Station with the quantity set to ∞" was fixed in v1.20.38, and saves stuck on it can continue. v1.21 also shows sale prices on the station recipe selection screen, and a progress border appears on the recipe icon while a maid cooks.

**Momo's wish step bug.** v1.20.38 fixed both "Double-check Momo's Cook task on the Chalkboard" and the infinite-quantity registration step not completing. Existing saves were not reset.

**Priorities.** Since v1.20, maids take on cooking stations for the pricier items on sale first.

**Practical suggestion.** Check chalkboard and station settings before assuming a wish is stuck; the two known blockers are patched as of v1.20.38.

**Automation.** The official notes checked here do not describe a hidden order-automation feature.
